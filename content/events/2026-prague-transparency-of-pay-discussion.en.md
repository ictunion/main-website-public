Czech Republic will soon implement a new change in the labor code, in following the European Pay Transparency Directive. We have been running a campaign on it and how the Czech government is (or isn’t) implementing it properly. We have asked workers to fill survey, submitted feedback and pushed back on weak proposals. We have been sending out our newsletters. Now we want to talk to you. In July we were in Brno, now we are in Prague: we are inviting you, Wednesday the 26th of August at 19h at the cozz office (Uruguayská 178/5, 10200 Prague 2, 2nd floor, cozz doorbell). If you plan to come, please fill out [this form](https://nextcloud.ictunion.cz/apps/forms/s/5ygymQCC4JCSbEfmcWdB4WZr) so we can make sure there is space and drinks for everyone :)

No formal presentation, just 5-10 minutes from us to set the context and then we want to hear from you!

What we will/can be discussing:
- Why pay transparency actually matters 
- What is in the EU directive
- What the Czech government got wrong in their proposal 
- What we are pushing for instead 
- What will change for you
- What happens next, and how you can get involved 

Do not expect a lecture. We are not here to tell you what to think. We are here to talk, to listen, and to figure out together what we can do. Bring questions. Bring frustration. Bring ideas.
