The gathering will take place **Thursday 27th of August from 6 PM (18:00)** in v [Přístavu 18600](https://18600.cz/kontakt/)

Our traditional Union BBQ is enriched this year by collabing with the Young Unionists, a group that gather workers from all kinds of fields. In ICT we have less of a union tradition than for example in education, healthcare and manufacturing. So this is a great opportunity to learn from each other! All in a chill atmosphere over a grill and some cold ones. The grill is BYOB (Bring Your own Barbecue). The grill is reserved by the unions, beer can be purchased on tap. Bring a little extra so we can swap some of our favorite delicacies.
