We would like to invite you to the Game Jam, that will take place on October 9–11, 2026 at the premises of the Střední škola umění a designu in Brno. Doors open at 17:00 and game jam starts at 18:00. Whether you work in the games industry, are a hobbyist, or are simply interested in game development, come make your own game with us while sharing your personal experiences and learning about what trade unions are and how they work. We want to create a space where you can share the hardships and joys of our industry in a creative way and try to find paths toward better conditions for everyone.

Entrance is free, but you need to sign up first by filling registration form: [https://nextcloud.ictunion.cz/apps/forms/s/iqGNmeL7BbwZbfgGbaadjJPf](https://nextcloud.ictunion.cz/apps/forms/s/iqGNmeL7BbwZbfgGbaadjJPf)

Capacity is limited to 25 people! We recommend signing up ASAP!
